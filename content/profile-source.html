    I am currently an Associate Professor (PhD supervisor) at the School of Cyber Security, Tianjin University, Tianjin, China. I received the B.E. degree from Hefei University of Technology, China, in 2017, and my D.E. degree from the same university in 2023, supervised by Prof.Meng Wang and Prof.Yu Gu. During my doctoral period, I receive a CSC scholarship, and I am guided by Prof.Yusheng Ji from National Institute of Informatics, Japan.

    My research lies at the intersection of wireless sensing, security, and ambient intelligence. My long-term goal is to build privacy-preserving ambient intelligence systems that seamlessly understand human behaviors and protect sensitive information in cyber-physical environments, particularly for aging societies and future human-machine ecosystems. We envision a future where wireless signals become an invisible sensing medium that enables ambient intelligence without cameras or wearable devices. Our mission is to develop physics-aware and privacy-preserving sensing techniques for healthcare, smart homes, and aging societies. I have published 30+ papers at top international conferences and journals, including IEEE S&P, USENIX Security, ACM MobiCom, IEEE JSAC, and ACM IMWUT/Ubicomp. I’m the recipient of the IEEE SMC Society Andrew P. Sage Best Transactions Paper Award, the IEEE HITC Distinguished Phd Dissertation Award, the IEEE ICME 2026 Outstanding Area Chair Award, and the IEEE ICME 2025 Best Paper Award Nomination.
